On ObjectOS Cloud we run the AI model for you, so the in-product AI — the
[AI Builder](/docs/build/ai-builder) and the assistant that answers questions
about your data — draws on an **AI allowance**. On a self-managed ObjectOS
Enterprise deployment you bring your own model instead, and none of this page's
limits apply: see [On ObjectOS Enterprise](#on-objectos-enterprise).

## One allowance for all AI work

Building an app, asking about your data and running actions through the
assistant all draw on the **same allowance**. There is no separate budget per
kind of work. Each request draws on it in proportion to the work the model does
for it, and a more capable model, where your plan offers a choice, draws more.

The allowance is counted per **environment**. **Home** in the cloud console
shows how much of this week's allowance has been used.

## Two rolling windows

Every plan has the same two limits, and both are **rolling**: usage counts for
a fixed length of time and then drops out, so room comes back continuously
rather than on a calendar date. Ruled on
[cloud#2059](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/cloud/issues/2059).

| Window | What it is | Free | Team and above |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7 days** | The budget: how much AI work fits in a week | One weekly allowance per environment; a hard limit | A larger allowance per AI seat, multiplied by the organization's AI seats; [credit packs](#buy-ai-credits) raise it |
| **5 hours** | The pace: how much fits in a short burst, so one runaway session cannot spend the week | Applies, but sits above Free's weekly allowance | Per AI seat, multiplied by the seats; credit packs do not raise it |

The exact sizes of the allowances are not published while ObjectOS Cloud is in
[Public Preview](/docs/editions/public-preview). Free's is sized for the whole
first experience: building an app, changing it several times, and still asking
it questions.

## When a limit is reached

The request is refused with HTTP `429`, and the chat shows a message that says
which limit it was and when it frees up:

| Situation | Code | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Free, weekly allowance used | `AI_DESIGN_QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` | *You've used this week's free AI quota. It resets on DATE — upgrade to keep going.* |
| Paid, weekly allowance used | `AI_ALLOWANCE_EXHAUSTED` | *Your AI allowance for this week is used up. It resets on DATE, or buy a credit pack to continue now.* |
| Any plan, five-hour pace reached | `AI_DESIGN_QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` on Free, `AI_ALLOWANCE_EXHAUSTED` on a paid plan | *You've hit the five-hour AI usage limit. It resets within five hours — then you're good to go.* |

The date is when the oldest usage still counted leaves the window; the error's
`details.resetsAt` carries the same moment as an ISO timestamp. Nothing else in
the environment is affected: your apps, data and integrations keep working.

## Buy AI credits

On a paid plan, an owner or admin can top up the weekly allowance with a
one-off **AI credit pack**: open the environment's **Settings → Plan and
billing**, then **AI credits** → **Buy AI Credits**. A secure checkout opens in
a new tab.

- A credit pack raises the **7-day** budget only. The five-hour pace is a rate
  limit and no purchase lifts it.
- Free does not take credit packs; the way past Free's weekly limit is the
  [upgrade to Team](/docs/cloud/plans-and-seats#upgrade).
- If credit packs are not on sale yet for your organization, the button says
  so instead of opening a checkout.

## Which model answers

Every plan answers with the **same default model**: paid plans differ from
Free by the size of the allowance, not by a stronger model. Where a choice of
models is offered to a paid environment, the AI chat lets you pick one, and a
more capable model draws the allowance down faster.

## AI through your own client is not metered here

An agent you connect over [MCP](/docs/configure/mcp) — Claude Code, Cursor and
the like — runs on **your** model and your own tokens. It does not draw on the
AI allowance. What bounds it is the environment's MCP request limit
([Storage and Limits](/docs/cloud/storage-and-limits#mcp-limits)).

## On ObjectOS Enterprise

A self-managed Enterprise deployment brings its own model and key, configured in
[AI Service](/docs/configure/ai), and pays its model provider directly. There
are no platform AI credits, no allowance and no metering: the per-AI-seat fee is
a license, not a usage bundle. How much AI work your deployment does is limited
only by your own provider account.
