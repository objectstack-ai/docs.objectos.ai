An environment on ObjectOS Cloud answers at a **platform subdomain**: a label of
your choice on the platform domain.

| | **Platform subdomain** |
|---|---|
| Looks like | `acme` on the platform domain |
| Plans | Every plan, Free included |
| How many | Add as many as you like |
| Certificate | Covered by the platform's own |

Platform subdomains are free on every plan, as ruled on
[cloud#1087](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/cloud/issues/1087).

ObjectOS Cloud does not offer pointing a domain you own at an environment.

## Change the address

Your environment's address is your organization's
[slug](/docs/cloud/organization#the-organization-slug) on the platform domain.
It is free on every plan, and so is any other branded label you choose.

To change it, open the environment's **Settings → Name and domain** and choose
**Change Hostname**. Type just the label: the platform domain is added for you.
A label is 4 to 63 lowercase letters, digits and dashes, with no dash at either
end and no two in a row, and a few reserved words are refused. It does not have
to start with your organization's slug.

## Add a second address

To add a second platform subdomain instead of replacing the first, use
**Add Domain** in the same section. The address shown on the environment's
**Overview** is the one to give people.

Sessions belong to one address: switching between two of them means signing in
again.
