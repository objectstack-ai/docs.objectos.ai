On ObjectOS Cloud everything you own belongs to an **organization**: its
environment, its apps and packages, its plan and its members. You create it
when you sign up ([Start on ObjectOS Cloud](/docs/start/cloud)), and you manage
it in the **cloud console** at [cloud.objectos.ai](https://cloud.objectos.ai).

## The cloud console

The console's sidebar is the map of this section:

| Sidebar item | What it holds | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | The organization at a glance: its environment, apps, members, this week's AI allowance | — |
| **Environments** | The organization's one environment, with its settings and lifecycle | [Environments](/docs/cloud/environments) |
| **Members** | The people in the organization and their pending invitations | this page |
| **Billing** | The plans compared, and the upgrade | [Plans and AI Seats](/docs/cloud/plans-and-seats) |
| **Advanced → Connected Runtimes** | Self-hosted servers linked to this organization | [Cloud Connection](/docs/cloud/cloud-connection) |
| **Advanced → Packages** | The packages your organization has published | [Apps](/docs/cloud/apps) |

The environment also has a console of its own, at its own
address. That is where your team uses the apps; the cloud console is where you
manage the organization around them.

## The organization slug

The **slug** is the organization's short identifier, chosen at sign-up. It is
also the address of your environment: the slug, followed by the
platform's domain.

- 4 to 30 characters: lowercase letters, digits and dashes.
- No dash at the start or end, and no two dashes in a row.
- A short list of reserved words is refused (`admin`, `api`, `www`, `docs`,
  `default` and others the platform uses itself).

An owner or admin can change the slug from the organization's record with
**Change Slug**, at most once every 90 days. Every platform subdomain of the
organization is rewritten to the new slug, and the old slug is held for 90
days so nobody else can take it in the meantime
([Domains](/docs/cloud/domains)).

## Members and invitations

Open **Members** in the sidebar. The page has two tabs: the members, with
their roles, and the invitations still pending.

Everyone in the organization is a member with their own ObjectOS Cloud
account. There are two ways in, and which one you use depends on who the
person is.

**Employees are added in the environment.** People who work in the
organization's apps are added by an administrator of the environment, in its
**Setup → Users** — see [Adding people on ObjectOS Cloud](#adding-people-on-objectos-cloud)
below. The **Members** page has an **Add employees** button that opens that
page in the production environment.

**Organization-level people are invited here.** Choose **Invite members**,
enter an email address and pick a role, for the people who manage the
organization or who do not work in the environment: admins, and members such
as billing staff or partners. They receive an invitation, and once they accept
it they are members of the organization. If the environment is not ready,
cannot be opened, or has no administrator who can sign in, you can also invite
an ordinary member here.

From the lists, whoever manages the organization can **change a member's
role**, **remove a member**, and **resend** or **revoke** a pending invitation.

### Adding people on ObjectOS Cloud

In the environment, open **Setup → Users**. An administrator of the
environment has two buttons there:

- **Add User** — one person: their email address and name, and optionally a
  position, a permission set and their manager's email address.
- **Import Users** — many people at once, pasted as CSV text: a header row,
  then one person per line, at most 500. The columns are `email`, `name`,
  `positions` and `permission_sets` (machine names separated by `;`), and
  `manager_id` (the manager's email address). Each row is added on its own, so
  a bad row fails alone and the result lists every row.

Each person is in the environment at once, with their position, permission
sets and manager, before they ever sign in, so records, approvals and
assignments can already name them. They have no password in the environment.
ObjectOS Cloud sends them an invitation to the organization as a **Member**,
recorded as yours. When they accept it and sign in with their ObjectOS Cloud
account, they land in the environment as the person you prepared.

Adding someone who is already a member sends no new invitation. On a
Developer Edition organization, a person beyond its member limit is refused,
row by row in an import.

### Accepting an invitation

The invitation email leads to ObjectOS Cloud, where the person signs in or
creates their account and accepts. **Accepting an invitation needs a verified
email address.** An account whose address is not verified yet cannot accept;
the invitation stays pending until the address is verified, and then the same
person accepts it. If you signed up with a password, open the verification
link in the email ObjectOS Cloud sent you when you signed up, then accept the
invitation again.

### Deactivating and reactivating people

People's day-to-day changes stay in the environment's **Setup → Users**:
positions, permission sets, managers.

- **Deactivating** a person there also removes them from the organization.
  Their user record in the environment and all their history stay. If they
  had not accepted their invitation yet, the invitation is withdrawn.
- **Reactivating** them sends the invitation again.
- An organization **owner** or **admin** cannot be deactivated from the
  environment. Change their role or remove them on the **Members** page first.

## Roles

A membership role decides what a person can reach in the organization. It is
not a permission set: what someone may do with records inside an environment
comes from that environment's [permission sets and positions](/docs/configure/permissions).

| Role | What it is for | Who can invite to it |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full control of the organization, its plan and its environment | An owner |
| **Admin** | Manages the organization: members, environment, billing | An owner or admin |
| **Member** | Uses the organization's environment and apps, or works with the organization without managing it (billing staff, partners) | An owner or admin; an environment administrator, by adding the person in **Setup → Users** |

Owners and admins are the people this section means by "whoever manages the
organization": they see the plan, the upgrade and the environment actions that
change something. A plain member sees the environment and opens it, but is
not offered actions they could not complete.

### Owners and admins administer the environment

The organization's owners and admins are the environment's administrators.
Nobody grants this in the environment: it follows the role on the **Members**
page.

- **Promote** a member to admin, and they become an administrator of the
  environment.
- **Demote** an admin to member, or **remove** them, and that administrator
  standing ends. Removing someone and inviting them back as a member does not
  bring it back.
- An environment administrator can make another person an administrator of
  the environment. That person stays a plain member of the organization, and
  their standing in the environment is not touched by role changes on the
  **Members** page.
- The standing that comes from the organization role cannot be taken away
  inside the environment. Change the role on the **Members** page instead.

**Open** on the environment enters each owner or admin as themself.

## Where sign-in lives

On ObjectOS Cloud every account is a cloud account. ObjectOS Cloud signs a
person in, and the environment receives them: what an environment holds for
each person is **authorization only** — their positions, permission sets and
sharing. Every hosted environment uses this single sign-in by default, so
people sign in once with their ObjectOS Cloud account and open the environment.
A plan that includes single sign-on with your own identity provider works
differently: the people that provider signs in hold no ObjectOS Cloud account,
so the environment keeps its sign-in page with both ways in (see
[Authentication](/docs/configure/authentication)). The rest of this section
describes the default.

- **Opening the environment signed out** goes straight to the ObjectOS Cloud
  sign-in page and, once signed in, back to the page that was opened.
- **The ObjectOS Cloud sign-in admits only members of the organization.**
  Someone who is not a member, or whose invitation is not accepted yet, is not
  let into the environment.
- **The environment has no password of its own.** Nobody signs in to a hosted
  environment with a local password, administrators included. Passwords,
  email verification and social sign-in all live in the ObjectOS Cloud
  account.

If nobody in your organization can sign in to its environment any more,
contact ObjectOS support.

**Taking an environment to your own infrastructure.** Because the accounts
live in ObjectOS Cloud and not in the environment, they do not travel with it.
On a self-managed deployment you either issue people new credentials there, or
bind it to your own identity provider so they sign in through it — see
[Authentication](/docs/configure/authentication).
