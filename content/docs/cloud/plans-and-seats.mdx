ObjectOS Cloud has three plans. The plan belongs to the **organization** and
applies to every one of its environments. Only **AI seats** are paid for:
everyone else in the organization uses and views apps for free.

| | **Free** | **Team** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $24 per AI seat / month, or $20 per AI seat / month billed yearly | By contract |
| Production environment | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| [Development environments](/docs/cloud/environments) | — | Two, plus one per AI seat | ✓ |
| Archived development environments kept | — | 30 days | A year |
| [AI allowance](/docs/cloud/ai-credits-and-limits) | A weekly allowance | A higher allowance that grows with AI seats, plus [AI credit packs](/docs/cloud/ai-credits-and-limits#buy-ai-credits) | Grows with AI seats, plus AI credit packs |
| [Storage per environment](/docs/cloud/storage-and-limits) | 1 GB | 20 GB | Not capped |
| [AI agent access (MCP)](/docs/configure/mcp#on-objectos-cloud) | 20 requests / minute, 1,000 / day | 120 requests / minute, no daily cap | 120 requests / minute, no daily cap |
| Branded platform subdomain | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Your own domain](/docs/cloud/domains) | — | One per production environment | ✓ |
| Approval workflows | — | — | ✓ |
| Sign-in | ObjectOS Cloud sign-in | ObjectOS Cloud sign-in | ObjectOS Cloud sign-in; single sign-on with your identity provider by contract |
| Support | Community | Standard | Priority |

ObjectOS Cloud is in [Public Preview](/docs/editions/public-preview):
prices are preview-period prices, and any change is announced in advance.

## The AI seat

An **AI seat** is a member who can build with AI and ask AI about your data —
the [AI Builder](/docs/build/ai-builder) and the in-app assistant. It is the
only paid seat. Members without one, viewers, and developers who build through
their own AI client over [MCP](/docs/configure/mcp) are not charged.

The number of AI seats you buy is a setting of the organization, chosen at
checkout and [changeable later](#change-ai-seats). It sizes three things:

- the organization's **AI allowance**, which is per seat
  ([AI Credits and Limits](/docs/cloud/ai-credits-and-limits));
- the **development environment pool**, two plus one per seat
  ([Environments](/docs/cloud/environments));
- the number of people who can be **assigned** an AI seat in an environment,
  where per-person seat assignment is switched on.

## Upgrade

Upgrading is done by an owner or admin, from an environment's page: its
header, or **Settings → Plan and billing**.

1. Choose **Upgrade Plan**. The dialog asks for the plan (Team), the billing
   cycle (monthly, or yearly at $20 per AI seat a month) and the number of AI
   seats.
2. A secure checkout opens in a new tab. The charge is to your organization,
   not to one environment.
3. When the payment completes, the organization's plan changes and every one of
   its environments picks up the new plan, with no redeploy.

While online payment is not yet open for your organization, the button reads
**Upgrade to Team** and opens **Billing** instead, where Team's
**Contact sales** button starts the upgrade with us.

Enterprise is sales-assisted: **Contact sales** on its column of **Billing**.

## Manage your subscription

A Team plan bought through the online checkout is a subscription, and an owner
or admin manages it from an environment's **Settings → Plan and billing**:
**Manage subscription**, on the **Current plan** row, opens Paddle's customer
portal in a new tab. In the portal you can:

- **cancel** the subscription. It stops renewing, and the plan stays until the
  end of the period you have paid for; then the organization returns to Free,
  and nothing is deleted ([Downgrading](#downgrading));
- update the **payment method**;
- see your **transactions** and download **invoices**.

While the subscription runs, the **Plan and usage** card on **Home** shows
owners and admins **Next charge:** and the date; after a cancellation it reads
**Cancels at period end:** and the day the plan ends.

**Manage subscription** is offered on the Team plan, to owners and admins, once
online payment is open. A plan billed by invoice has no online subscription to
manage: Business and Enterprise never show the button, and a Team plan billed
by invoice answers that it has none. Change or cancel such a plan through
[sales@objectos.ai](mailto:sales@objectos.ai).

## Change AI seats

The console cannot change the number of AI seats yet, and the customer portal
changes neither the seat count nor the plan. To add or remove AI seats, an
owner or admin writes to [sales@objectos.ai](mailto:sales@objectos.ai) with the
organization and the number of seats it should have, and we change it on your
subscription. Once it changes, the AI allowance, the development environment
pool and the seat assignment cap all follow the new count.

## Payment, invoices and refunds

Purchases in the console — the Team plan and
[AI credit packs](/docs/cloud/ai-credits-and-limits#buy-ai-credits) — are sold
by **Paddle.com**, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle runs the
checkout, calculates any sales tax or VAT, and issues the receipt and the
invoices, so the charge on your card statement and the receipt name **Paddle**
rather than ObjectOS. Invoices are in the customer portal
([Manage your subscription](#manage-your-subscription)).

When a payment can be refunded, and how to ask for it, is set out in the
[Refund and Cancellation Policy](https://www.objectos.ai/en/refunds/) on
www.objectos.ai.

## See what your plan leaves out

Two places in the console tell you, without a sales page in between:

- **Billing** compares the three plans side by side, and marks the one you
  are on with **Current plan**. Every member can read it; only owners and
  admins see its buttons.
- An environment's **Overview** tab has a collapsed line,
  **Features that need a higher plan: N · View**. Opening it lists each
  feature the current plan does not include, with the plan that carries it.
  Where a locked feature appears elsewhere in the console, it carries a short
  mark naming its plan, such as **Team plan and above**, which leads to
  **Billing**.

## Downgrading

Dropping to a lower plan never deletes data:

- Development environments keep running; Free only refuses creating or
  restoring one ([Environments](/docs/cloud/environments#after-a-downgrade-to-free)).
- An environment over the Free storage allowance keeps every byte, stays
  readable and exportable, and stops taking uploads and imports
  ([Storage and Limits](/docs/cloud/storage-and-limits)).
- Your own domains are paused, not released, and come back by themselves on a
  later upgrade ([Domains](/docs/cloud/domains#downgrade-and-upgrade)).

## Business

**Business** is a contract option of ObjectOS Enterprise on the hosted
service, sold by [sales](/docs/editions/license) rather than from
**Billing**. On ObjectOS Cloud it adds approval workflows and a 90-day
retention window for archived development environments. Its price and what
the contract covers are on [License & Pricing](/docs/editions/license).
