## One product, three editions

ObjectOS is the **official runtime environment for ObjectStack applications**,
in three editions: **ObjectOS Community** (open source, self-hosted, free),
**ObjectOS Cloud** (we operate it) and **ObjectOS Enterprise** (you operate
it, under a commercial license). ObjectStack is the name of the protocol,
SDK, CLI and framework the three editions run.

The open-source story has two names and one license. The
**[ObjectStack framework](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack)**
(Apache-2.0) is the protocol, kernel, SDK and CLI; **ObjectOS Community** is
the open-source runtime and Console UI built from it — the production
runtime image, the MCP server, permissions and sharing, audit, every
protocol capability — everything you need to **build, run, and self-host
your own applications**, free, with no feature gate between development and
production. Its code lives in the same objectstack repository. ObjectOS
Cloud and ObjectOS Enterprise are what you buy when you want the **embedded
AI** and an **officially operated / supported** platform on top of that
mechanism.

## Editions

ObjectOS Community is free. The two commercial editions share one unit of
pricing, the **AI seat**: ObjectOS Cloud sells it self-serve, ObjectOS
Enterprise by annual contract.

ObjectOS Cloud is **Free** and **Team**; there is no Business plan and no
Enterprise plan. Team has no cap on members, records or objects.
**ObjectOS Enterprise** is one edition, always a private deployment on your
own infrastructure. It is licensed per AI user, billed annually, with a
minimum of 25 AI users, and sold by contract with invoice payment. There is
no card checkout and no public price:
[contact sales](mailto:sales@objectos.ai).

Want to self-host **for free**? That's **ObjectOS Community** (MCP-only AI:
bring your own model). See [ObjectOS Community](#objectos-community) below.

**Open mechanism, closed intelligence.** Everything you need to model data and
run apps is open source (ObjectOS Community, on the ObjectStack framework).
Cloud and Enterprise layer on the *embedded intelligence* — the in-product AI
Builder and the "ask your data" assistant, governed and audited — plus
operational scale and official support.

**You pay only for AI seats.** The only billed seat is the **AI seat**, one
per user of the in-product AI; viewers and app users who don't use the AI
are free, with no per-user seat tax (a clean contrast to the $150–300/user
incumbents). On Cloud, developers building over MCP are free too. On
Enterprise, an agent that connects over MCP with its own API key is a
service identity, and each one counts as one AI user.

## Cloud or self-managed

Self-managed ObjectOS is **ObjectOS Community** (free) or **ObjectOS
Enterprise** (licensed). Cloud is Free and Team; Enterprise is always
self-managed.

- **Cloud (Free / Team)** — fully hosted, self-serve. The price includes
  hosting and AI usage (we run the model), within limits per environment:
  see [AI Credits and Limits](/docs/cloud/ai-credits-and-limits).
- **Enterprise** — licensed ObjectOS on your own infrastructure: one
  edition, a private deployment, sold by contract.
  - **Every feature is included**, whatever the size of the deployment: the
    in-UI AI, AI governance, hierarchy security, SCIM on by default,
    customer-managed keys, air-gapped licensing and data residency. You
    **bring your own model / key**: you pay your own LLM cost, with no
    platform AI bill or metering.
  - **Priced per AI user**, billed annually, with a minimum of 25 AI users.
    The license does not limit the number of nodes. A deployment runs on one
    node today, with backup and restore; high availability across several
    replicas is in development and is not offered until it ships.
  - **Bought by contract only**, with invoice payment; there is no card
    checkout. What changes the price is agreed in the contract: priority and
    SLA support, air-gapped deployment, a multi-year price lock, and a
    perpetual license.
  - **When a subscription ends**, the deployment falls back to Community:
    the Enterprise features switch off, and the system and your data keep
    working. Only a perpetual license, bought outright by contract, keeps
    the versions released while its maintenance was paid.

There is **no per-node charge**.

## FAQ

### Is ObjectOS open source?

**ObjectOS Community** is: the runtime and Console are Apache-2.0, free, and
developed in the open in the
[objectstack repository](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack),
alongside the ObjectStack framework they are built from. **ObjectOS Cloud**
and **ObjectOS Enterprise** are the commercial editions — the embedded AI,
hosting and operations, and support on top of that same mechanism. Single
sign-on with your identity provider is part of the open mechanism, free.

### Can I use ObjectStack in a closed-source SaaS I sell to my customers?

Yes. Apache-2.0 has no copyleft and no royalty — you don't need to share
your code, and you owe nothing if your product makes money.

### Can I self-host ObjectOS without paying?

Yes — that is **ObjectOS Community**: the same open mechanism, self-hosted,
with MCP-only AI (bring your own model). The *licensed* self-managed edition
is ObjectOS Enterprise, which adds the in-product AI, AI governance and
commercial support.

### Does the software stop working if I don't renew?

It never bricks. When a subscription ends, the deployment falls back to
Community: the Enterprise features switch off, and the system and your data
keep working. Keeping a version after the end, a version lock, comes only
with a **perpetual license**, bought outright by contract: it keeps the
versions released while its maintenance was paid. Three configurations that
need a license to exist at all refuse to start instead; see
[Licensing](/docs/deploy/licensing#when-a-license-ends).

### Does ObjectOS phone home?

ObjectOS Enterprise validates its license online; air-gapped Enterprise
licenses validate offline. ObjectOS Community has no telemetry, no license
check, and no update ping.

### Can I buy commercial support for a self-managed deployment?

Commercial support, with priority and SLA options, comes with an ObjectOS
Enterprise license: [contact sales](mailto:sales@objectos.ai). ObjectOS
Community is supported by the community; see
[Support](/docs/resources/support).

### Will ObjectOS Community and the framework always be free?

Yes. Apache-2.0 grants are irrevocable, and everything already released
stays licensed as released.
