## One product, three editions

ObjectOS is the **official runtime environment for ObjectStack applications**,
in three editions: **ObjectOS Community** (open source, self-hosted, free),
**ObjectOS Cloud** (we operate it) and **ObjectOS Enterprise** (you operate
it, under a commercial license). ObjectStack is the name of the protocol,
SDK, CLI and framework the three editions run.

The open-source story has two names and one license. The
**[ObjectStack framework](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack)**
(Apache-2.0) is the protocol, kernel, SDK and CLI; **ObjectOS Community** is
the open-source runtime and Console UI built from it — the production
runtime image, the MCP server, permissions and sharing, audit, every
protocol capability — everything you need to **build, run, and self-host
your own applications**, free, with no feature gate between development and
production. Its code lives in the same objectstack repository. ObjectOS
Cloud and ObjectOS Enterprise are what you buy when you want the **embedded
AI** and an **officially operated / supported** platform on top of that
mechanism.

Two **contract options under ObjectOS Enterprise** sit between Team and a
full private deployment, priced as before: **Business** (SSO and AI

Want to self-host **for free**? That's **ObjectOS Community** (MCP-only AI:
bring your own model). See [ObjectOS Community](#objectos-community) below.

**Open mechanism, closed intelligence.** Everything you need to model data and
run apps is open source (ObjectOS Community, on the ObjectStack framework).
Cloud and Enterprise layer on the *embedded intelligence* — the in-product AI
Builder and the "ask your data" assistant, governed and audited — plus
operational scale and official support.

**You pay only for AI seats.** The only billed seat is the **AI seat**;
viewers, non-AI app users, and developers building over MCP are free, with no
per-user seat tax (a clean contrast to the $150–300/user incumbents).

## Cloud or self-managed

Self-managed ObjectOS is **ObjectOS Community** (free) or **ObjectOS
Enterprise** (licensed). Cloud is Free and Team; everything above Team is an
Enterprise contract.

- **Cloud (Free / Team)** — fully hosted, self-serve. The price includes
  hosting and a generous monthly AI allowance (we run the model).
- **Enterprise** — licensed ObjectOS, sold by contract, in three shapes:
  - **Business** — a contract option on ObjectOS Cloud: SSO / SAML and AI
    governance (action audit, guardrails, permission scoping) on the hosted
    service, $54 / AI seat / mo or $45 / AI seat / mo billed annually,
    priority support.
  - **Business Self-Managed** — the single-node entry option: the Business
    plan delivered as a **single-node Docker license** for **10–25 AI seats**
    ($540/AI seat/yr, annual): in-UI AI and AI governance on your own
    infrastructure, **bring your own model / key** (you pay your own LLM
    cost — no platform AI bill or metering), card-paid, standard support. If
    the license lapses, the runtime keeps running and your data stays intact —
    the paid features simply switch off (it never bricks).
  - **Full private deployment** — for everything beyond that: clustering /
    HA / multi-node, multi-organization self-host, data residency and
    **air-gapped** operation, SCIM and identity lifecycle, compliance
    attestations, SLA and dedicated support, and offline / invoice payment.
    Sold as an **annual license** with multi-year price-lock options; on
    non-renewal your deployment **keeps the last paid version perpetually**
    (you lose updates and support, never your system or data). Pricing is
    volume-based — [contact sales](mailto:sales@objectstack.ai).

There is **no per-node charge** — clustering / scale is folded into
Enterprise.

## FAQ

### Is ObjectOS open source?

**ObjectOS Community** is: the runtime and Console are Apache-2.0, free, and
developed in the open in the
[objectstack repository](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack),
alongside the ObjectStack framework they are built from. **ObjectOS Cloud**
and **ObjectOS Enterprise** are the commercial editions — the embedded AI,
hosting and operations, SSO and support on top of that same mechanism.

### Can I use ObjectStack in a closed-source SaaS I sell to my customers?

Yes. Apache-2.0 has no copyleft and no royalty — you don't need to share
your code, and you owe nothing if your product makes money.

### Can I self-host ObjectOS without paying?

Yes — that is **ObjectOS Community**: the same open mechanism, self-hosted,
with MCP-only AI (bring your own model). The *licensed* self-managed edition
is ObjectOS Enterprise, and both of its options (Business Self-Managed on a
single node, or a full Enterprise deployment) add the in-product AI, AI
governance and commercial support.

### Does the software stop working if I don't renew?

It never bricks. Business Self-Managed degrades to the free-mechanism
behavior (paid features switch off; your data and runtime keep working).
A full Enterprise deployment keeps the **last paid version perpetually** —
you lose updates, new AI capabilities, and support, not your system.

### Does ObjectOS phone home?

ObjectOS Enterprise validates its license online; air-gapped Enterprise
licenses validate offline. ObjectOS Community has no telemetry, no license
check, and no update ping.

### What if I want a contractual warranty on ObjectOS Community?

That's the **ObjectOS Runtime Subscription** — a support contract on
ObjectOS Community (official distribution, SLA, security-patch priority)
with zero feature difference. Contact
[sales@objectstack.ai](mailto:sales@objectstack.ai).

### Will ObjectOS Community and the framework always be free?

Yes. Apache-2.0 grants are irrevocable, and everything already released
stays licensed as released.
