**ObjectOS Enterprise** is ObjectOS under a commercial license, running on
infrastructure you operate: the in-product AI, AI governance and official
support on your own servers, and — on a full private deployment —
clustering, several organizations on one deployment, SCIM and air-gapped
operation. See [Compare Editions](/docs/editions/compare) for the full list
and [License & Pricing](/docs/editions/license) for the contract options,
from single-node **Business Self-Managed** up.

## 1. Get a license

ObjectOS Enterprise is sold by contract:
[contact sales](mailto:sales@objectstack.ai). Your license comes with
registry credentials for the licensed Enterprise image, and each release's
notes name the exact image digest to run.

## 2. Deploy

The deployment guides start at [Deployment](/docs/deploy), which walks the
three decisions every deployment makes. Then pick your shape:

- [Docker](/docs/deploy/docker) — the supported stack: pull, verify, license,
  run, scale, upgrade.
- [Kubernetes](/docs/deploy/kubernetes) — the properties any orchestrator
  must preserve.
- [Air-gapped](/docs/deploy/air-gapped) — air-gap as a license mode, and the
  combinations refused at startup.

## 3. Operate

[Production Readiness](/docs/operate/production) is the checklist before you
go live; [Operate](/docs/operate) covers backup, upgrades, observability and
audit logs from there.

Evaluating before you buy? [ObjectOS Community](/docs/start/community) runs
the same runtime, free, and what you build on it is ordinary ObjectStack
metadata that moves to Enterprise unchanged.
