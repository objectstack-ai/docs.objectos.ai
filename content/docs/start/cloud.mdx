**ObjectOS Cloud** is ObjectOS hosted and operated for you: you work in the
browser, and there is nothing to install or deploy. The **Free** plan is
enough to build your first app. ObjectOS Cloud is in
[Public Preview](/docs/editions/public-preview).

Want it on your own machine instead? That is
[ObjectOS Community](/docs/start/community). On your own infrastructure,
under a license? [ObjectOS Enterprise](/docs/start/enterprise).

## 1. Sign up

Create your account on the
[ObjectOS Cloud sign-up page](https://cloud.objectos.ai/_console/register).
Already have one? [Sign in](https://cloud.objectos.ai/_console/login).

## 2. Create your organization

Your organization is the workspace your team shares: its apps, its data and
its members. Give it a name and a **slug** — the short identifier ObjectOS
uses for it.

## 3. Create the production environment

An organization runs its apps in an environment. Create its **production
environment**; that is where your first app lives. On the Free plan,
production is the only environment — see
[Environments](/docs/cloud/environments).

## 4. Build your first app

Two ways, and you can use both:

- **Describe it to the [AI Builder](/docs/build/ai-builder)** — *"I need to
  track support tickets with priority and assignee"*. It proposes the objects,
  fields and views as a change you approve before it runs.
- **Install a [template](/docs/build/templates)** and start from a working
  app.

Developers have two more routes: build in source and publish a
[package](/docs/build/packages#two-developer-routes-on-objectos-cloud), or let
their own agent build over [MCP](/docs/configure/mcp#build-with-an-agent) as
drafts a person publishes. Either way the result is ordinary ObjectStack
metadata: REST APIs, generated screens, permissions and audit come with it. See [Build](/docs/build) for
everything you can add next.

## 5. Invite your team

Invite the people who will use the app. Only **AI seats** are billed:
viewers and people who use apps without the in-product AI are free. See
[Organization and Members](/docs/cloud/organization) for invitations and roles,
[Users](/docs/configure/users) for access inside an environment, and
[Permissions](/docs/configure/permissions) for who sees what.
